About ReWear

A marketplace for clothes that deserve a second life.

Why we built this

Most of us have clothes we don't wear. A jacket that never quite fit, shoes worn twice, a dress from one wedding. They sit in the back of the wardrobe because throwing them out feels wrong and selling them feels like too much effort.

Meanwhile, the fashion industry produces more than 100 billion garments a year, and a large share of them are worn only a handful of times before being discarded.

ReWear exists to close that gap. List what you're not wearing, find something you'll actually use, and keep good clothes in circulation instead of in landfill.

What we care about

Keeping clothes in use

The most sustainable garment is one that already exists. Every item resold here is one that doesn't need to be made.

Making it simple

Listing an item takes two minutes. No fees, no haggling, no shipping labels to print.

Looking after members

Contact details stay private. We connect buyers and sellers ourselves, and we're here if something goes wrong.

The team

ReWear was built by five computer science students at The College of Management Academic Studies, as a final project for a full-stack development course.

It runs on Node.js and Express with a MongoDB database, and it was written from scratch — from the data models to the design.
