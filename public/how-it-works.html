The points system

One point equals one shekel. Sell a ₪120 dress, get 120 points.

Points are how value moves around ReWear. Instead of sending money back and forth, what you earn from selling goes straight back into your next purchase. It keeps clothes circulating — which is the whole idea.

Common questions

How do I get the item I bought?

Pickup is arranged directly between you and the seller. After your order goes through, our team contacts you with the seller's details so you can agree on a time and place. Buyers and sellers don't see each other's contact details on the site — everything goes through us.

Can I use points and a card together?

Not right now. Each purchase uses one method — either your card or your points. If you don't have enough points for an item, the points option is greyed out and shows how many more you'd need.

Can I turn my points into money?

No. Points can only be spent on ReWear. That's deliberate: it's what keeps items moving between members instead of leaving the community.

What if something's wrong with my order?

Get in touch and we'll sort it out. Orders can't be cancelled from your account, because by then the item may already have changed hands — so we handle each case individually.

How many items can I list?

As many as you like. Each item can have up to four photos.

Is it free?

Yes. No listing fees and no commission — sellers keep the full value of every sale in points.
